# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, collaborative, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and professional.
- Provide actionable feedback and credit the work of others.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, spamming, or disruptive conduct.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please email the maintainers at security-audit@example.com with relevant details. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community.
